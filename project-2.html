Office Bids

A full stack application utilizing SupaBase and React.js to allow employees to contribute to office relocations.

Live Link (shutdown)

Project Overview

This project was inspired by my previous co-workers concern over moving office locations, where they felt it was a quite uninvolved process for them when they wanted to contribute and make choices. Noting how the office move felt like they were just told to pack everything up and move offices. I then realized how this is happing all of the time, a friend of mine has even moved offices 4 times in the last year!

What Office Bids is, is a spin off on an auction website where users would be asssigned a certain amount of credit (in dollars) from there managers, and could then use that credit to do 1 of 2 things: add a new office item for other to contribute to, or contribute to others ideas. This would allow for the employes to get a say in what would be in there new office and even add a bit of a game/competition to the office where people would be trying to get eachother to bid on their items.

An example of this would be if Employee A wanted to get a new coffee machine for the office. Employee A would then find a price, image, and link to the coffee machine of Employee A's choice. They would upload this to OfficeBids (as seen above) where a new tile would appear (as seen below) on the main page for the other employees to see. Employee A could put some of their credits towards it, but if the machine was $1000 and each employee was given $100, Employee A would have to try to advertise this choice and get atleast 9 others to contribute to it before it could be marked as complete.

During my Internship with the Solution Stack (they were about to move offices), I pitched this idea to my supervisor, who to my absolute surprise, wanted to try it out. He then helped me finish everything up and make sure everything entered would be secure, and it was used by them in July of 2024 where it was a huge success!
